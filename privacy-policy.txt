Privacy Policy for Ad Muter

Effective Date: August 22, 2025

Ad Muter is a Chrome extension that automatically mutes ads on supported video websites like YouTube and Dailymotion. We take your privacy seriously and want to clearly explain what information we do — and do not — collect.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

Personal information (name, email, etc.)

Browsing history

Cookies or trackers

Any identifying data about you or your usage

2. Permissions Explained

To function correctly, Ad Muter requires certain Chrome permissions. Here’s why:

activeTab / tabs: To mute and unmute audio only on the tab you’re currently watching.

host permissions: To access supported video sites (like YouTube, Dailymotion).

scripting & webNavigation: To detect and mute ad content as needed.

storage: (Optional) May be used to save minor user preferences. No personal data is stored.

3. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected, there's no risk of exposure or misuse. All processing happens locally in your browser.

4. Third Parties

Ad Muter does not share data with any third-party services or advertisers. We are 100% independent and ad-free.
